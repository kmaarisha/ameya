Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This policy explains how Ameya ("Ameya", "I", "we") collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit ameya.agency, contact us or work with us. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000. It also covers the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for visitors from those regions.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Ameya is the Data Fiduciary (the "controller" under GDPR) for personal data collected through this website. You can reach us at maarisha@ameya.agency or +91 91378 75412.

2. What we collect

Information you give us: your name, business name, email, phone number, the services you're interested in, and anything you write in the enquiry form, email or WhatsApp.

Information collected automatically: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, referring site and time spent. We only collect this through cookies or similar technologies if you have agreed to them (see section 4).

We do not ask for sensitive data such as financial details, health information, religion or political views.

3. Why we use it

We use your personal data only for clear, specific purposes:

—To reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal (based on your consent).
—To deliver services we have agreed to provide (to perform our contract with you).
—To understand how the website is used and to measure our own advertising (only with your cookie consent).
—To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We never sell your personal data, and we won't send you marketing emails unless you have opted in.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising tags

Essential cookies keep the site working. With your consent, we may also use:

—Analytics (e.g. Google Analytics) to see which pages are useful.
—Advertising tags (e.g. Google Ads, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag) to measure our ads and show relevant ads to past visitors (remarketing).

You can accept, refuse or withdraw consent at any time through the cookie banner or your browser settings. You can also manage ad personalisation in Google Ad Settings, Meta Ad Preferences and LinkedIn Ad Settings. Refusing cookies will not stop the site from working.

5. Our advertising standards

Every campaign we create or manage, for ourselves or for clients, follows the Code for Self-Regulation of Advertising Content of the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI), the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the advertising policies of Google, Meta and LinkedIn. In practice, that means:

—Ads are honest and truthful, and any claims can be backed up with evidence.
—Paid, sponsored and influencer content is clearly labelled, in line with ASCI's influencer guidelines.
—No misleading offers, fake urgency or other dark patterns, in line with the CCPA Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023.
—No targeting of children, and no targeting based on sensitive characteristics such as health, religion, caste or sexual orientation.
—Customer lists uploaded to ad platforms are used only where the people on them gave valid consent.

6. Client data

When we run campaigns, social accounts or websites for a client, we process their customers' data only on the client's instructions and under a written agreement. We act as a Data Processor in these cases. The client, as Data Fiduciary, is responsible for getting any consent needed. We keep that data confidential, use it only for the agreed work, and return or delete it when the work ends.

7. Who we share it with

We only share data with trusted service providers who help us run the business, such as website hosting, email, form tools, analytics and ad platforms. They are bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations. We may also disclose data where the law or a lawful authority requires it. Some providers store data outside India. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards the law requires, such as standard contractual clauses for EU/UK data.

8. How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to a project are deleted within 24 months. Client and billing records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Analytics data is kept in line with each platform's retention settings, for no longer than 26 months. When data is no longer needed, or you withdraw consent, we delete it.

9. How we protect it

We use reasonable security measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, two-factor authentication on accounts and limiting who can see your data. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

10. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

—ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
—have it corrected, completed, updated or erased;
—withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it;
—raise a grievance with us, and escalate it to the Data Protection Board of India if you're not satisfied;
—nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf.

If you're in the EU or UK, you also have GDPR rights to data portability, to restrict or object to processing, and to complain to your local supervisory authority. To use any of these rights, email us. We will reply within 30 days.

11. Children

This website and our services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has shared data with us, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and we'll let you know directly about any significant changes.
